## Decisions of Section 9A

| ID | Decision |
|---|---|
| D-058 | Three design variables, **one-factor-at-a-time** with three levels: V ±10 %, q″ ±10 %, t 8 / 10 / 12 mm. The baseline is the central level of every series |
| D-059 | Support sensitivity is a **separate** study at P00: S1 (current, solved), S2 (laterally held, solved), S3 (fixed–pinned, proposed). **No support stiffness is assumed.** Required stiffness is reported as a design requirement |
| D-060 | Thickness cases hold the **total heat input Q** constant (q″·D_o constant) so that the geometric effect is not confounded with a heat-input change |
| D-061 | Mesh rebuild rules M-1 … M-5 and the y⁺ policy (measured y⁺_max ≤ 1, mean ≤ 0.5; reuse if the estimate is ≤ 0.8) |
| D-062 | Operating ranges are limited by the frozen property tables with margin: near-wall air ≤ 590 K for acceptance in screening; no temperature outside a table at any iteration in 9B. Extending the air table is not permitted in 9B |
| D-063 | A pipeline-control case C00 (P00 re-run through the parametric automation) must reproduce P00 before any parametric result is accepted |

## Decisions of Section 9B-1

| ID | Decision |
|---|---|
| D-064 | Every CFD case runs through **one** journal (`solve_param.py`). It loads P00's converged case settings, loads the case mesh by `mesh.replace`, applies only the case's intended change, and requires (a) a zero settings diff after the replace and (b) a second full settings-tree diff containing exactly the approved paths. C00 validates the whole path |
| D-065 | V cases change the inlet velocity and the turbulence intensity derived from it (0.16 Re^−1/8, as approved in 9A). The **initial field stays P00's** (300 K, w = 23.5 m/s), so the settings diff has only the two approved paths; a converged steady solution does not depend on its start |
| D-066 | Thickness flux derived from the actual heated area: q″ = Q_P00 / A_heated = 8000 · 40 / Do (8,888.9 / 7,272.7 W/m²). It holds Q on both the CAD (π·Do·L) and the 48-facet CFD mesh, verified in Fluent (602.7552 W in both T cases) |
| D-067 | Validity gate after every iterate call: all monitored temperatures inside the air (250–600 K) and Inconel (293.15–673.15 K) tables at every iteration, and no limiter, reversed-flow or divergence message. A violation stops the case, which is recorded as failed and never rescued by changing physics. No case triggered it |
