# Preliminary hazard review: liquid-nitrogen concept

**Status: preliminary and conceptual. CONCEPTUAL — NOT APPROVED FOR CONSTRUCTION. This is not a completed HAZOP, not a safety certification and not an engineering approval.** It ranks nothing, assigns no risk level and sets no tolerable-risk criterion. It was drafted with AI assistance for the repository owner and has not been reviewed by a qualified cryogenic or safety engineer. A facility would need a formal hazard study with the real design and site before any physical implementation.

## 1. Scope and method

Scope: `CRYOGENIC_SYSTEM.md`, its schedules and diagrams. **Liquid nitrogen only**; liquid oxygen, liquid hydrogen, propellants, combustion and hazardous operating trials are excluded. The method is a structured screening: for each hazard the initiating condition, the consequence, the proposed concept-level safeguards, what must be verified and what stays unresolved. Safeguards are proposals with tags; **none is sized or rated**. Numbers quoted come from the screening calculations, which rest on placeholder assumptions (AS-10 to AS-16) and illustrative geometry (IL-12 to IL-15), and are for scale only.

## 3. What this review does not do

It does not rank or score hazards, set performance levels for the trip, size relief devices or vents, calculate hydraulic transients, or claim that any proposed safeguard is adequate. It is not a substitute for the owner's safety review or for the relief, vent and stress calculations that need the unresolved inputs of `missing_inputs_register.csv`.
