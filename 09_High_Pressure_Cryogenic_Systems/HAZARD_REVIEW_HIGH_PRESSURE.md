# Preliminary hazard review: high-pressure gaseous-nitrogen concept

**Status: preliminary and conceptual. CONCEPTUAL — NOT APPROVED FOR CONSTRUCTION. This is not a completed HAZOP, not a safety certification and not an engineering approval.** It ranks nothing, assigns no risk level and sets no tolerable-risk criterion. It was drafted with AI assistance for the repository owner and has not been reviewed by a qualified pressure-systems or safety engineer. A facility would need a formal hazard study (for example a HAZOP with the real design, operating procedures and site) before any physical implementation.

## 1. Scope and method

Scope: `HIGH_PRESSURE_SYSTEM.md`, its schedules and diagrams. Nitrogen only; no oxidizer, fuel, combustion, toxic fluid or hazardous trial. The method is a structured screening: for each hazard the initiating condition, the consequence, the proposed concept-level safeguards, what must be verified, and what stays unresolved. Safeguards are proposals with tags; **none is sized or rated**. Numbers quoted come from the screening calculations and are for scale only (illustrative case, section 3 of `HIGH_PRESSURE_SYSTEM.md`). A **regulator is not overpressure protection** and an instrumented trip does not replace a relief device (NASA-STD-8719.17D 4.10.1.10 and 4.10.3.2, read; no compliance claimed).

## 3. What this review does not do

It does not rank or score hazards, set safety integrity or performance levels for the trip, size any relief device, or claim that any proposed safeguard is adequate. It is not a substitute for the owner's safety review or for the relief and vent calculations that need the unresolved inputs of `missing_inputs_register.csv`.

## 4. Unresolved actions

Failure positions (UI-13), relief set pressures, capacities and discharge locations (UI-07, UI-11), regulator data (UI-08), component data (UI-09), materials for the cold gas (UI-10), room volume and ventilation (UI-12), classification and code (UI-05), the decision on CV-301 (HAZ-HP-09), and any flexible connection (HAZ-HP-12).
