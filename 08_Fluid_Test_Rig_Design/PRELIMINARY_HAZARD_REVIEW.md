# Preliminary hazard review

**Conceptual and educational. Not a HAZOP, not a risk certification and not a safety approval.** It lists credible off-normal conditions for the concept in `SYSTEM_DESCRIPTION.md` so that the design carries explicit safeguards and open questions. It assigns no severity or likelihood and does not claim that the safeguards are sufficient.

## 1. Scope

The rig is a non-hazardous air system: a low-pressure blower, air at about 300 K up to the test article and about 369 K after it, a free-jet discharge to atmosphere, and a heated tube whose surface reaches about 563 K (CFD peak 562.58 K, M039). Compressed-gas storage, cryogenic fluids, propellants, combustion and hazardous gases are out of scope. It is not a rocket-engine or launch-pad ground system.

## 2. Method

For each item: the initiating condition, the possible consequence, the concept-level safeguard (with the tags it uses) and the residual design question. The safeguards correspond to requirements in `REQUIREMENTS_TRACEABILITY.csv`. Where a number is quoted it comes from `calculation_results.json` or the existing project data.
